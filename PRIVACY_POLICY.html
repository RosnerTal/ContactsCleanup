Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We build the Unused Contacts Cleaner app as a Free app with premium lifetime in-app purchases. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Information Collection and Use

Our app accesses sensitive on-device data in order to function, specifically:

  • Contacts (Read/Write): To display your address book, allow search, classify keep/delete tags, and perform batch deletions.
  • Call Logs and SMS (Read): To passively analyze timestamps and total interaction logs locally to determine contact inactivity levels.

Data Local Processing & Privacy

CRITICAL SECURITY ASSURANCE: All processing, filtering, classification, and scanning of contacts, call logs, and SMS is performed strictly locally on your device. We do not host external database servers. We never collect, transmit, upload, sell, or share your contacts or any personal information to external servers or third parties.

Security

We value your trust in using our application. Since no data leaves your physical device, your contact details remain secured under the native sandbox security boundaries of the Android operating system.
