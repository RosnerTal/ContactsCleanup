Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: June 2026

The Unused Contacts Cleaner application operates strictly locally on your device. We do not maintain any cloud databases, accounts, or user profiles on remote servers.

How to Delete Your Data

Since all data (contact classification, app preferences, and interaction statistics) is stored exclusively on your device, you can delete all of this data at any time by performing either of the following steps:

Method 1: Clear App Storage

  1. Open your Android device's Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps -> All Apps.
  3. Select Unused Contacts Cleaner.
  4. Tap on Storage & cache.
  5. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data) and confirm. This permanently purges all local app data.

Method 2: Uninstall the Application

  1. Press and hold the Unused Contacts Cleaner app icon on your home screen or app drawer.
  2. Tap Uninstall (or drag the icon to the Uninstall zone) and confirm. This automatically deletes the app sandboxed database and all local metadata.
